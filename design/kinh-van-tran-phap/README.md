### Động phủ

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original vertical mobile game-like cinematic environment illustration for a Vietnamese cultivation journal, full bleed 2:3. Camera at floor level in a vast surreal scripture hall belonging to no existing franchise. Architecture is formed by suspended uneven ivory paper panels and towering hanging scrolls running upward beyond the image, dense black dry-brushed calligraphic marks on the panels as atmosphere. A tiny anonymous ivory-robed traveler seen from behind, just right of lower center, standing before a blade-thin vertical opening of bright daylight. Long angled shadows across the blank pale stone, warm paper grain, deep almost neutral ink blacks, cool neutral gray haze, off-white and ivory, a tiny touch of muted vermilion from a square seal on one sheet. Intense scale contrast, luminous side light, slight drifting dust, textured painterly cinematic concept art, not flat watercolor and not glossy 3D. Reserve calmer dark paper on left upper third and low-detail charcoals in extreme lower quarter for future Vietnamese text overlay. No jade, green or teal. No ornate gold borders, no generic pagoda, no overlaid title, no readable UI, no logo or watermark. Original composition.

Predominantly neutral near-black, slightly warm shadow, refined very fine texture visible at close inspection, extremely quiet and even from top to bottom for small off-white UI numbers. ### Đột phá

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original full-bleed vertical 2:3 cinematic rank ceremony illustration for a Vietnamese cultivation journal. In a vast black-ink void, a tiny anonymous dark-robed cultivator from behind stands on a narrow black stone ledge near bottom center, gazing into a vast suspended ritual diagram tilted away in three-dimensional space. Three sharply defined warm gold thin circles each of distinct diameter, a few flowing broken dry-brush arcs and discreet angular incisions, ring fragments disappear into soft black fog at corners. The ring center burns candle-ivory, fine gold particles float around, subtle neutral charcoal fog, minute paper dust. Upper and bottom edges stay deep calm black. Strong light hierarchy and scale, painterly material surface, controlled saturation, sophisticated concept art. No jade, green, teal, text overlay, copied character, franchise elements, logos or watermark.

### Nét mực rời

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: compositing layer for hand-painted moving scripture in a Vietnamese cultivation journal. Real transparent canvas. Tall 2:3 canvas with three spaced, loosely staggered vertical streams of broken, gestural dry-brush strokes, rendered in warm pale ivory and soft gray, a few tiny near-white floating paper fibers. Elegant East Asian calligraphic rhythm as abstract marks with no identifiable written words, most strokes small, intermittent and individually separated. Strongly irregular rhythm, soft alpha at ink edges and much genuine transparent space, especially left third. Gentle cinematic floating atmosphere, no gray or black background, no plane, no textures outside the marks, no fog bank, no gradients in alpha away from marks, no green, no logo.

### Mẫu icon

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original style exploration sheet for five small navigation icons in a Vietnamese cultivation gym journal. One wide, very clean row of exactly five separated monoline silhouette marks, evenly spaced, same optical scale and stroke weight, evident at 24px. Left to right: a shrine doorway with a curved eave for home; a pair of stone training weights linked by a short bar for workout; a tied rolled manual for plans; a folded bound book with a brush mark for journal; a rising line made of three simple stepped peaks for progress. Bold warm ivory ink brush and chisel texture at outer edges, mostly solid clean strokes with just enough chipped edges, art direction of an engraved lacquer seal, perfectly plain near-black backdrop, restrained tiny warm vermilion accent only on the book tie if needed. No boxes, titles, text, extra icons, swatches, hatching or green. Intended only as a coherent visual reference, no interface mockup. Landscape wide.
